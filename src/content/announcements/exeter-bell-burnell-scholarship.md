Featured in a [University of Exeter news article](https://news-archive.exeter.ac.uk/2021/may/articles/universityofexeterstudent.html)  news article following my award of the **2021 Bell Burnell Graduate Scholarship Fund**, I discuss my passion for astrophysics and my research on the role of atmospheric aerosols in shaping exoplanet climates and their observable signatures. I share my academic journey, the importance of understanding planetary atmospheres, and how this scholarship will support my doctoral research into the climates and habitability of planets beyond our Solar System.
