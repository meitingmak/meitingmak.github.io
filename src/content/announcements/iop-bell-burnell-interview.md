Featured in an **Institute of Physics** interview as a recipient of the 2021 Bell Burnell Graduate Scholarship Fund, I discuss what drives my research on exoplanet atmospheres and why understanding haze is critical for interpreting planetary observations. I share what drew me to physics, the challenges I’ve faced, and how this scholarship will support my journey studying atmospheric dynamics in planets beyond our Solar System.
