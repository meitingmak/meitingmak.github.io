I was recently featured by the **Croucher Foundation** in *[Peering Through the Haze](https://croucher.org.hk/en/news/peering-through-the-haze)*. The piece highlights my research on atmospheric haze in exoplanets, exploring how these tiny suspended particles shape the circulation and climate of distant worlds and affect how we observe them through telescopes. I discussed the challenges of modelling haze using 3D models and how my work aims to bridge the gap between complex simulations and what we can detect from real observations.
